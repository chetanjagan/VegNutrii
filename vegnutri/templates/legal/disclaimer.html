Health disclaimer

{{ app_name }} provides general nutrition information for healthy adults. It is not medical advice and doesn't replace a doctor or a registered dietitian.

Talk to a professional first if you

  • are pregnant or breastfeeding;
  • have diabetes, kidney, liver or heart disease, thyroid conditions, PCOS or any other medical condition;
  • take medicines that interact with food (for example blood thinners or diabetes medication);
  • have, or have had, an eating disorder;
  • are planning meals for a child.

About our numbers

Calorie and protein targets are estimates from standard formulas (Mifflin-St Jeor). Food values are approximate, based on Indian food composition data, and real dishes vary with recipe and portion. Micronutrient comparisons use ICMR-NIN 2020 reference intakes and are not a substitute for blood tests.

Fasting

Vrat plans follow common religious fasting practice. They aren't a weight-loss method. If you have a medical condition, check with your doctor before fasting.

If something feels wrong

Stop and seek medical help if you feel unwell, faint or unusually tired. In an emergency call 112.
